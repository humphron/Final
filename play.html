Play

Play board games @ Free Parking

Find your new favorite game, choosing from their extensive collection.

Interact @ Activate Games

Work with a team to complete challenges in a variety of game rooms. Dress in athletic wear and get ready to put your mind and body to the test.

Practice your pool @ Roc's House of Billiards

Hone your craft or just relax with a friendly game of pool. Play with the regulars and learn a thing or two.

Take fun photos @ Selfie Cincy

Pose in front of different sets and get all the pics you need for your social media.

Solve puzzles and escape @ Breakout Games

You're locked into a mysterious room and you have to use clues to figure out how to escape in the time limit.
