Relax

Quiet the mind @ Enhancing Your World Holistic Recovery and Wellness Center

Float in a sensory deprivation tank and find that all your thoughts suddenly become crystal clear.

Make a custom candle @ The Candle Lab

Sniff dozens of scents from their candle wall and make your own combination to be poured in a new candle.

Taste a latte flight @ Wholy Beans Coffee Co.

Grab a seat and a friend and enjoy a latte flights with unique, seasonal flavors.

Enjoy nature inside @ Khron Conservatory

View real bonsai trees, a waterfall, and many beautiful plants inside Khron's greenhouses.

Observe space @ Cincinnati Observatory

Take a look through the huge telescopes and see what you find!
